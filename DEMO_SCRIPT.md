# INDIE Protect Demo Script

Target length: 2 minutes 30 seconds.

## 0:00-0:15 - Context

"Insurance is usually easy to buy and difficult to understand when it matters.
INDIE Protect is a bank-native protection cockpit that connects discovery,
purchase, policies, renewals, claims, and assistance in one experience."

Show the Home screen.

## 0:15-0:35 - Protection Score

"Ananya sees a Protection Score of 64, her motor renewal due in ten days, and
the most important action first. The dashboard is designed around protection
decisions rather than a product catalogue."

Scroll through the renewal card, active policies, and recommendation.

## 0:35-1:00 - Discover And Compare

Open Explore and choose Motor.

"Recommendations are matched to Ananya's car and protection needs. The ranking
is based on fit, not only commission or price."

Open Compare.

"The comparison keeps insured value, add-ons, exclusions, and annual price in
the same surface. The higher premium is translated into a monthly difference so
the trade-off is visible."

## 1:00-1:20 - Purchase And Policy Vault

Choose Drive Total and complete the simulated purchase.

"Checkout reuses verified bank information while still requiring explicit
confirmation. The new cover appears in the policy vault beside external
policies, so the customer has one lifecycle view."

Open Policies.

## 1:20-1:55 - Guided Claim

Open Claims and start a motor-damage claim.

"The claim journey asks for the incident first, then explains which evidence is
needed and why. After submission, Ananya sees the claim owner, next action, and
status timeline instead of an unexplained reference number."

Submit the claim and advance the status once.

## 1:55-2:15 - Assistance And AI

Open Protect AI and use the FIR question.

"The assistant explains approved policy information in plain language and
escalates when confidence is low. Roadside assistance and telemedicine are also
available from the same protection context."

## 2:15-2:30 - Close

"The MVP begins with motor and health, measured by Monthly Protected Customers:
people who complete a meaningful protection action. The recommendation is to
pilot renewal and claims with a small INDIE cohort, prove trust and service
levels, then expand."

End on the Home screen or title slide.

## Recording Setup

- Record at 1920 x 1080 or 1280 x 720.
- Use the desktop prototype at 100% browser zoom.
- Hide bookmarks and notifications.
- Use a clean demo state before recording.
- Keep the pointer still while speaking.
- Export as MP4 at 1080p when possible.
